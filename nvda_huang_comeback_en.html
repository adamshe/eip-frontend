1. Conclusion

A comeback (A or B, defined below): about 14% by 2027-12-31 and about 32% by 2029-12-31 (calculated, the sum of A and B).

Judgment of this report: Jensen Huang (below, the former CEO) is unlikely to return to Nvidia; if he does come back, the more likely route is to lead or found another company, and only after the independent directors' investigation and the congressional line have produced a result (inferred). The company's announcement does not state the reason for the departure, and this report does not discuss the reasons given in media reports; this site's report of 2026-11-13 contains a scenario analysis, and this report gives only the probabilities for its own question.

Probabilities of the three paths (all inferred; the three are mutually exclusive, and the probability of A and B both holding is treated as 0, set by this report):

  • Path A, returning to Nvidia as chief executive officer, executive chairman or director: about 4% by 2027-12-31 and about 7% by 2029-12-31 (inferred).
  • Path B, leading or founding another company valued or capitalized at no less than US$10 billion: about 10% by 2027-12-31 and about 25% by 2029-12-31 (inferred).
  • Path C, neither A nor B (includes holding no post and acting only as a shareholder, or leading a company valued below US$10 billion): about 86% by 2027-12-31 and about 68% by 2029-12-31 (calculated, the remainder of the first two). Within this, the probability that he publicly votes or speaks against the board is about 5% by 2027-12-31 (inferred).

Path A is low because of where decision power sits: the company has a single class of common stock with no super-voting rights (reported); he also resigned as a director (confirmed); his holding is about 3.58% (reported, as of 2026-03-23); and the board has appointed its first board chair and started a search (confirmed). Of the 7 comparable cases, only 1 returned to the original company, a reversal by a non-listed company within 5 days of the departure (reported); this departure is now 3 days old, and the board's actions point the other way (calculated; confirmed).

Path B is higher than Path A because of his base: his holding is worth about US$144.7 billion at the 2026-11-12 close (calculated), and California law voids non-compete clauses (reported). Path B is not high through 2027 because of constraints and time: two rounds of congressional letters (confirmed), the securities class action (reported) and the independent directors' investigation (reported) are all unresolved; among comparable cases only 1 new company reached a valuation of US$10 billion, taking about 4.5 years (calculated).

Comparable cases n = 7, fewer than 10, so the base rate only reaches suspect (suspect = grounds exist but the evidence is not enough to confirm). The readings that would change the judgment first are: whether the independent directors' investigation publishes a conclusion, whether the new Congress continues the inquiry on 2027-01-03, whether the terms of the separation agreement are disclosed, and when a permanent CEO is appointed.

2. Method: who are the enemies, who are the friends

This report uses Mao Zedong's thought as its analytical lens: it first classifies each party by economic position and interest, then looks at the principal contradiction, the united front, base areas and the stage of the protracted war (set by this report).

"Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society" (1925) opens by making the distinction between enemies and friends the first question of the revolution, and its criterion is each party's economic position and interest, not slogans. In this report, "the supporting side" means the side whose goal is "he returns to a position that can steer Nvidia or another large AI company". Enemy, friend, middle or dual character is assigned by each party's interest in his return from today onward, not by its role in the departure; the same party can be classified differently on the three paths, so the table of enemies and friends lists each path separately.

The other four tools are used as follows. On Contradiction: find the principal contradiction of each path and the principal aspect of the contradiction, and state under what conditions it turns. United front: whom to unite, whom to win over (middle forces and parties with a dual character), whom to isolate. Base area: what he still holds and what constrains it. Protracted war (On Protracted War): the three stages of defense, stalemate and counteroffensive, each with dates and the observable condition for moving to the next stage. Seeking truth from facts: every judgment carries a status word; confirmed = a company or government document, reported = media or a third party, calculated = computed by this report from source numbers, estimated and inferred = this report's judgment, not found = this report did not find it.

4. Three paths and who decides

Judgment of this report: for Path A the decision sits with the board and the institutions holding the majority of votes, not with him; for Path B it sits with capital providers and legal constraints; for Path C, the shareholder-only part is his own decision, but his influence is limited by a voting power of about 3.58% (reported; inferred).

Path A has two channels. The first is a board invitation: a majority of the board can decide an appointment, and the current board has just accepted the resignation, set up a chair and started a search (confirmed), while the independent directors lead an investigation (reported). The second is a proxy contest: the company has a single class of common stock (reported), the supermajority provision has been removed (reported), and the initiator must persuade the institutions holding the majority of votes. As of 2026-06-30, the top ten institutions together hold 33.45% and passive index holders no more than 23.34% (calculated); his 3.58% is about one ninth of the top ten institutions combined (calculated). Passive money usually votes with the board and the proxy advisers' recommendations (inferred), so the threshold for Path A is the institutions, not his shares.

Path B has two forms: being hired to lead an existing company valued at no less than US$10 billion, which needs that company's board to agree; or founding a new company that reaches a financing valuation of US$10 billion before the settlement date, which needs capital providers (set by this report). Legally, California Business and Professions Code section 16600 voids non-compete clauses, and since 2024-01-01 this extends to contracts signed outside California (reported); trade secrets are not affected, non-solicitation terms are a separate matter, and the terms in this case were not found.

Path C is neither A nor B (includes holding no post and acting only as a shareholder, or leading a company valued below US$10 billion). The shareholder-only part needs nobody's consent: a holding below 5% means public statements and voting do not require a prior beneficial ownership filing (inferred); but 3.58% cannot decide the result of any single vote on its own (calculated).

6. Principal contradiction and its principal aspect

Judgment of this report: today the principal aspect of all three paths lies on the side of unresolved legal and political matters, not on the side of ability or capital; once those matters are settled, the principal aspect for Path B moves to capital and talent, and for Path A to institutional holders (inferred).

Path A: the principal contradiction is between a return and the decisions the board has already made, the ongoing investigation and the search; the principal aspect is the board, because the appointment procedure and dates are in its hands (inferred). Condition for turning: the investigation's conclusion is public with no company-level findings, and the search drags on; only then does the principal aspect move to institutional holders; if a permanent CEO is appointed, Path A is essentially closed (inferred).

Path B: the principal contradiction is between the capital and talent available and the unresolved congressional requests, the investigation, the lawsuit and the undisclosed separation terms; today the principal aspect is on the constraint side (inferred). Condition for turning: Congress stops naming him personally, the investigation closes, and the separation terms are disclosed without a long non-solicitation; this report estimates the earliest time is after the new Congress takes office on 2027-01-03 (estimated).

Path C (shareholder-only part): the principal contradiction is between a voting power of about 3.58% and all the other votes, and the principal aspect lies with institutional holders (reported; inferred). This contradiction does not turn over time; it changes only with the size of the holding.

11. What this means for readers following the company

Judgment of this report: for Nvidia, the main path, Path C, means the company runs without a founder in office, and the continuity of the succession and the product roadmap is the first checkpoint (inferred).
