Nvidia after the CEO's resignation: five links, the timing, and four scenarios

This report judges that through 2027-06-30 Nvidia most likely lands in Scenario 2, the slow grind, at about 56% (inferred); the probability of a slow grind genuinely caused by a transfer of pricing power is no more than 40% (inferred; a probability over all outcomes, not 40% of the 56%); the probability that third-quarter revenue at the 2026-11-17 earnings is at least USD 108.0 billion is 85% (inferred); after the resignation the congressional committees still sent a second round of letters (confirmed), and whether the government track has moved to channels and licensing is only doubtful.

1. Conclusion

This report's judgment: after the resignation of Nvidia's chief executive, the most likely outcome through 2027-06-30 is Scenario 2, the slow grind, at about 56% (inferred). This report tests three judgments: first, that the slow grind is the main path and is caused by a transfer of pricing power; second, that the government track did not stop with the departure and has moved to channels and licensing; third, that the timing of the resignation is not random.

The four scenario probabilities (observation window 2026-11-11 to 2027-06-30): Scenario 2 Slow grind 56%, Scenario 1 Intact 12%, Scenario 3 Channel and licensing tightening 23%, Scenario 4 Enforcement against the company itself 9% (all inferred). The slow grind means no enforcement event, gross margin and valuation multiple declining step by step, and the company gradually becoming an ordinary large company. The 56% is the probability of filing the outcome by observable events; it includes the mild case in which gross margin misses the threshold only because of memory costs and no transfer of pricing power is seen. The probability of a slow grind genuinely caused by a transfer of pricing power is no more than 40% (inferred; a probability over all outcomes, not 40% of the 56%), because that causal chain requires Link 1 (substitution, 80%, inferred) and Link 2 (pricing power, 40%, inferred) to hold together, so the smaller of the two is taken. The 40% is a ceiling, not another scenario. Link 1 holds within the window if a competing supplier's next-quarter AI semiconductor revenue guidance is at least USD 24 billion, or non-Nvidia contracts quoted in gigawatts keep being added; Link 2 holds within the window if, at least once through 2027-06-30, the gross margin guidance midpoint is no higher than 72.5% while the supply commitment balance does not rise (set by this report).

The 2026-11-17 earnings: the probability that third-quarter revenue is at least USD 108.0 billion is 85% (inferred); what matters is the midpoint of next-quarter gross margin guidance and how the company attributes it. The government track did not stop with the departure: on 2026-11-12 the committees sent a second round of letters (confirmed); whether it has moved to channels and licensing is only doubtful (doubtful = some basis, but the evidence is not enough to confirm).

Comparison with the market on three cells (calculated): the market side is a rough calculation with flat implied volatility, and the two downside cells show no disagreement; with a real skew the market gives the downside a higher probability, so this report is not more bearish than the market on the downside (estimate). The disagreement is only on the upside: the price leaves an upside probability that this report does not accept (doubtful). Timing: the resignation's event date and the deadline for answering Congress are the same day, 2026-11-11 (confirmed), and the earnings come 6 days later (calculated). The baseline for how often ordinary quarters coincide in this way was not found by this report, so "the timing of the resignation is not random" is an inference and is only doubtful.

Probabilities are this report's inferences, without a model or a sample; data run to 2026-11-12; the three-year path is only an estimate. A check of sell-side consensus, the option chain of 2026-11-13, the former chief executive's filings on changes in shareholdings, and any enforcement against the company itself were not found by this report. Sources for factual figures are in the source table at the end; items marked inferred are this report's judgments, and items marked calculated are computed by this report from source figures.

How to separate cost from pricing power. Gross margin falling while the commitment balance still rises and delivery times do not shorten is cost; gross margin falling while commitments stop rising and receivables turnover lengthens is pricing power (inferred). The forecast quarter-on-quarter rise in regular DRAM contract prices has slowed from 58% to 63% in the second quarter to 10% to 15% in the fourth (reported; both are forecasts, actual values were not found by this report), but prices are still rising, so cost cannot be ruled out as the explanation now; only if gross margin guidance still does not recover after contract prices turn down can the decline not be attributed to cost alone (inferred).

Elasticity. A fall in gross margin from 75.0% to 71% to 72% lowers earnings per share by about 4.5% to 6% (calculated).

6. Why Now

This report's judgment: the resignation falls on the same day as a deadline already set (confirmed). Three House committees wrote on 2026-11-04, asking the former chief executive to answer by 11-11 and attend a closed briefing on 11-18; the event date is exactly 11-11 (confirmed). The earnings are on 11-17 and the 10-Q deadline is 12-04 (confirmed; calculated). The signing rules for the quarterly-report certification and the baseline for how often ordinary quarters coincide were not found.

Who benefits: only categories are listed, and this report did not find the dollar size for any of the three (inferred). Large customers: the amount gained from price concessions was not found; the counter-test is that supply is still constrained (reported, 2026-08-27), and a change of leadership is also a risk to deliveries to large customers. Competing suppliers: the amount of orders newly added because of this event was not found; the counter-test is that the publicly announced non-Nvidia contracts all predate the company's guidance of 2026-08-26 (see Section 3) and were not added after the event. Parties arguing for tighter export licensing to China: there is no dollar amount to count; the counter-test is that the rule of 2026-01-15 changed licensing to China to case-by-case review, which is a loosening (confirmed). Benefiting is not the same as driving. Sell-side downgrades are concentrated on 2026-11-10 to 11-12, all after the board statement of 2026-11-08 (reported), so the sell side follows and does not lead.

Who has the capacity: those able to set a public date on their own are the congressional committees (by virtue of their authority) and the board. The congressional track did not stop with the resignation: the second round of letters of 2026-11-12 widened the recipients to the company's general counsel and the Under Secretary of Commerce, and a press release the same day asked for the immediate passage of four chip export bills (confirmed). Whether it has moved to channels and licensing is not sufficiently evidenced: the first letter was a request and did not mention a subpoena (confirmed, 2026-11-04); whether the second round mentions a subpoena was not found; enforcement against the company itself was not found; the current Congress's term runs to 2027-01-03 (confirmed). The timing coincides with deadlines already set, the baseline was not found by this report, and "not random" is only doubtful (inferred); this report attributes the timing to no party.
